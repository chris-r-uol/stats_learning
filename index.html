Practise the statistics, one step at a time

Work a question, type your answer, and find out straight away whether it is right — and if it is not, what went wrong. Everything is worked in Excel, with R and Python alongside if you prefer them.

How this works

Do the work in your spreadsheet

This site checks your thinking; it does not replace Excel. Download the data, do the calculation properly, then bring the answer back here.

Wrong answers are the useful ones

Common mistakes are recognised individually. If you use STDEV.P instead of STDEV.S, the site says so rather than just marking you down.

Nothing is recorded anywhere

None of this counts towards your assessment, and your answers never leave your browser. Guessing costs you nothing but the learning.
